# Gate A Tester Rules

## Purpose
Gate A is a product-workflow test, not a nutrition study and not a medical-allergy product launch.

## Alpha Family #1
The founder household should use the build first for at least one real planning cycle before any outside household is invited.

## First 5–10 outside households
Prefer households that:

- pack school lunch multiple days per week;
- have at least one K–5 child;
- currently experience some lunch-planning friction;
- are willing to log what came back for at least several lunches;
- can use a nickname rather than a child's full name.

## Exclude initially
Until the product-level allergen audit and safety review are complete, do **not** recruit families who need the app to manage a medically significant food allergy. The alpha can exercise restriction logic internally, but generic food concepts are not a substitute for current packaged-food labels.

Also avoid recruiting users who expect:

- exact grocery pricing;
- calorie or medical nutrition therapy;
- clinician-directed diets;
- a barcode scanner;
- school cafeteria planning;
- breakfast/dinner planning.

## What testers are actually testing

1. Is onboarding short enough?
2. Does the first generated week feel usable?
3. How many swaps are needed?
4. Is the grocery list useful?
5. Will a parent log what came back?
6. Does Week 2 feel more personalized than Week 1?
7. Does the workflow reduce weekly planning effort?
